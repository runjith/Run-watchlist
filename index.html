        About

        A working notebook.

        RUN Watchlist is a personal equity research workspace. Every company moves from research to thesis, valuation, decision, action and review — so hours of reading end in a clear, recorded view.

        01

        One workspace per company

        Thesis, checklist, valuation, decision, catalysts, risks, open questions, tasks, reviews and documents live together in one place.

        02

        Research stays independent

        Each research document is its own HTML file. This site links to them. It never rewrites them.

        03

        Thinking is never overwritten

        Every thesis version, valuation snapshot and decision is kept, so it is always possible to see what I thought six months ago.

        Note. This is a private notebook for recording my own research and decisions. It calculates simple arithmetic such as upside and downside, but it never recommends a decision. Nothing here is investment advice.
