3. What MM Forgings Actually Does

MM Forgings is a vertically integrated steel forger and machinist. It takes steel, forges it under high pressure into high-integrity components, and increasingly machines those components itself to capture more value.

The core advantage of forging over casting is grain structure and fatigue strength — critical for safety and power-train parts that experience repeated stress (crankshafts, axle beams, connecting rods, steering knuckles, gear blanks).

How money is made: Paid per part. Steel-price changes are generally passed through to customers (with lag). Higher machined content improves realisation and margins. Capacity utilisation is the single biggest swing factor because fixed costs (presses, power, labour, depreciation, interest) are high.

Key subsidiaries (AR FY26 & Forensic):

  • DVS Industries — wholly-owned crankshaft machining subsidiary; NCLT-sanctioned amalgamation into parent (June 2026 scheme).
  • Suvarchas Vidyut — wholly-owned electrical / alternator components start-up.
  • Abhinava Rizel (88% owned) — EV powertrain (motors, controllers, gearboxes). Acquired Sep 2022 for ₹15.84 Cr equity. Outstanding loan ₹63.50 Cr as of 31 Mar 2026. Cash burn ~₹1 Cr/month as of Q3 FY26 call. First SOP / ramp-up stage as of Aug 2026 call.

Sources: Business Overview, Annual Report FY25-26, Forensic Report, conference calls

14. Business Quality Assessment

Is this a good business?

Forging of safety-critical and power-train parts carries real switching costs once a supplier is qualified on a platform. That is a moderate moat. Vertical integration into machining further raises the barrier for pure-play competitors.

However, the business remains highly cyclical (CV + US truck), capital intensive, and exposed to steel and power costs. Pricing power is limited on a pass-through basis; volume and utilisation drive most of the P&L swing.

Overall quality assessment: Moderate — solid industrial franchise with genuine technical barriers, but returns have been diluted by heavy, slow-to-earn capital expenditure and cyclical end markets. Quality will only improve if the installed capacity starts generating acceptable ROCE.

17. Final Investment Conclusion

1. Investment thesis in one sentence: A capital-intensive forging franchise with genuine technical barriers that has spent heavily on capacity; the investment works only if the new press and volume recovery convert unproductive capital into rising ROCE and free cash flow.

2. Strongest arguments FOR:

  • Critical, high-switching-cost components in CV power-train / chassis.
  • Recent sequential improvement in sales and management tone (Q3 FY26 → Q1 FY27).
  • Large press, if successfully ramped, offers material revenue and operating leverage (management cites ~₹300 Cr potential).
  • Green-power and interest-cost initiatives provide identifiable margin levers.

3. Strongest arguments AGAINST:

  • ROCE already compressed to ~9%; half the recent large capex still unproductive.
  • Repeated timeline slippage on the flagship press and on Abhinava production.
  • Elevated debt, weak historical cash conversion (0.63×), and ongoing subsidiary cash burn.
  • Current valuation (13.6× EV/EBITDA, 28× P/E) already embeds recovery optimism.

4. What management has promised vs delivered: Promised capacity-led growth and mix improvement; delivered partial volume recovery but persistent delays on the physical assets that were supposed to drive the next leg of earnings. Guidance credibility is mixed with a clear optimistic bias on large-project timelines.

5. Biggest unresolved questions:

  • When will the 16,500-ton press generate its first meaningful commercial revenue, and at what run-rate?
  • Will ROCE exit single digits within the next 4–6 quarters?
  • Can absolute debt start declining while still funding machined-content growth and any further Abhinava needs?

6. What needs to happen over the next 2–3 years: Press fully ramped, utilisation materially higher, ROCE rising, debt stabilising or falling, Abhinava either contributing or ring-fenced without further large parent funding.

7. What could permanently impair the thesis: Prolonged low utilisation of the new capacity, a multi-year CV/US downturn before returns improve, or a significant further rise in leverage / related-party exposure.

Overall assessment: Requires More Evidence

This is a classic “show-me” industrial situation. The assets are largely in place or nearly in place; the earnings power and return on capital are not yet visible. The conference-call narrative has improved from the Q2 FY26 low, but physical delivery of the large press remains the single most important swing factor. At current multiples, the margin of safety for further execution slippage is limited.
