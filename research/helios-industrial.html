Research Helios Industrial Systems

Order book inflected.
Margin recovery is the question.

Ticker HELIO Sector Industrials Updated 18 Sep 2026

Helios supplies precision motion control and grid-scale automation to utilities and large industrial customers. After three flat years, the order book inflected sharply in Q2 FY26 on the back of grid capex. The stock has re-rated. The open question is margins.

Thesis

What I think is happening. Grid capex in the company's two core geographies is entering a multi-year upcycle driven by interconnection queues and substation replacement. Helios is one of three credible suppliers at the required reliability tier. The backlog gives unusual visibility into FY27–FY28 revenue.

What I'm watching. Gross margin. The Q2 revenue beat was driven by a mix shift toward lower-margin systems integration work. If that mix persists, revenue growth will not translate to EPS growth at the rate the market is implying.

Risks

  • Mix. Continued integration-heavy mix compresses margin.
  • Concentration. Top three customers are ~44% of revenue.
  • Working capital. Receivables cycle extended by 12 days YoY.
  • Valuation. At 34x FY27E, the stock prices in successful margin normalisation.

Valuation

At current price, the market is ascribing roughly 34x FY27E earnings. Historical five-year median is 22x. The re-rating is justified by order-book visibility, but not by current margin trajectory. I would want to see two consecutive quarters of margin expansion before arguing the multiple is sustainable.
